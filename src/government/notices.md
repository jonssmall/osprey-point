Notices below are posted in addition to publication in the *Sedgemere County
Record*, as the town charter requires. Each stays posted until the action is
final or the notice period closes.

## Notice of public hearing — Ordinance 2026-11

The Town Council will hold a public hearing on **Tuesday, August 18, 2026 at
7:00 p.m.** in Council Chambers, 220 Marsh Landing Road, on Ordinance 2026-11,
amending Chapter 14 of the town code to establish a short-term rental license,
an annual fee, and an occupancy limit of two persons per bedroom plus two.

The full text is available from the Town Clerk and on the meeting agenda.
Written comment may be submitted to the Clerk through 4:00 p.m. on August 18.

*Posted July 22, 2026.*

## Invitation to bid — street resurfacing, FY2027

Sealed bids for the FY2027 street resurfacing program (approximately 3.8 lane
miles, mill and overlay) will be received by the Town Manager's office until
**2:00 p.m., September 3, 2026**, at which time bids will be opened publicly.

Bid documents are available from Public Works for a non-refundable $50. A
pre-bid meeting will be held at the Dredge Road operations yard at 10:00 a.m. on
August 20. Attendance is not mandatory but is strongly encouraged.

The town reserves the right to reject any and all bids and to waive
informalities.

*Posted August 5, 2026.*
