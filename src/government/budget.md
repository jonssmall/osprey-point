Osprey Point operates on a fiscal year running July 1 through June 30. The
Council adopted the FY2027 budget on June 16, 2026 by a vote of 4–1.

## FY2027 at a glance

| Fund | Adopted | Change from FY2026 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| General Fund | $18,240,000 | +3.8% |
| Water & Sewer Enterprise | $6,105,000 | +6.2% |
| Stormwater Utility | $1,472,600 | +1.1% |
| Parks Special Revenue | $1,595,000 | +2.4% |
| **Total, all funds** | **$27,412,600** | **+4.1%** |

## Where general fund revenue comes from

| Source | Amount | Share |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Real property tax | $9,850,000 | 54% |
| Income tax distribution | $3,420,000 | 19% |
| Licenses, permits, and fees | $1,935,000 | 11% |
| Intergovernmental grants | $1,640,000 | 9% |
| Charges for services | $890,000 | 5% |
| Other | $505,000 | 3% |

The real property tax rate is **$0.5820 per $100 of assessed value**, unchanged
for the fourth consecutive year. Assessments rose an average of 4.1 percent
townwide, so the average residential bill increases about $71 despite the flat
rate.

## Five-year capital plan

The capital improvement plan totals $19.2 million through FY2031. The largest
commitments are the Sedgemere Creek filtration plant upgrade ($7.4 million across
three phases) and the Old Mill stormwater improvements ($4.1 million, beginning
FY2028).

## Debt and reserves

Outstanding general obligation debt stands at $21.6 million as of June 30, 2026,
well within the charter limit of 12 percent of assessed value. The unassigned
general fund balance is $4.35 million, or 23.8 percent of general fund
expenditures — above the Council's 15 percent policy floor.

## Audits and reports

The town's annual comprehensive financial report is prepared by an independent
auditor and presented to the Council each December. The FY2025 audit was
received with no findings and no management letter comments.

## Utility bill assistance

The FY2027 budget includes a $40,000 assistance fund for households below 200
percent of the federal poverty level. Applications are handled by the Finance
Department; see [Utility Billing](/services/utility-billing/).

## Getting the documents

The adopted budget, the five-year capital plan, and the annual audit are
available from the Finance Department at (609) 555-0120 or through a
[public records request](/services/records-requests/).
