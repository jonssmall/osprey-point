## Summary

Work sessions are for discussion only; no votes were taken.

**Present:** Mayor Marchetti, Council President Okonkwo, Vice President
Villanueva-Park, Council Member Sarabhai. **Absent:** Council Member Brandt
(excused).

## Short-term rental data

Staff returned with the analysis Vice President Villanueva-Park requested in
July. Of 84 identified short-term listings in town, 61 would meet the proposed
occupancy limit as advertised, 19 would exceed it, and 4 could not be determined
from listing data alone.

Discussion centered on whether the one-year grandfather period is enough for
owners who bought specifically to operate. The Council asked staff to draft
alternative language with an 18-month period for review at the August 18
session.

## Comprehensive plan scope

Planning & Zoning presented the workshop structure and the online comment
window. Council Member Sarabhai asked that the comment form be usable on
mobile and available in Spanish. Staff confirmed both.

Mayor Marchetti asked whether workshop attendance data would be published.
Staff will include attendance and demographic summaries in the plan appendix.

## FY2028 capital preview

Finance walked through preliminary FY2028 capital requests totaling $6.8
million against roughly $4.1 million in projected capacity. The largest items
are the Old Mill stormwater improvements ($2.4 million) and the second phase of
the filtration plant upgrade ($1.9 million).

Council President Okonkwo asked Finance to model the debt service impact of
issuing $3 million in FY2028 versus splitting the issuance across FY2028 and
FY2029.
