## Minutes

**Call to order.** Council President Okonkwo called the meeting to order at
7:02 p.m. Present: Mayor Marchetti, Council President Okonkwo, Vice President
Villanueva-Park, Council Member Brandt, Council Member Sarabhai. Also present:
Town Manager Hollins, Town Clerk Achterberg, Town Attorney (remote).

**Approval of minutes.** The minutes of the June 16, 2026 regular session were
approved as presented, 5–0.

## Public comment

Six residents addressed the Council.

Four spoke on the proposed short-term rental ordinance. Three supported an
occupancy cap, citing parking and late-night noise on Bay Street. One, a
property owner on Pintail Lane, asked that existing operators be grandfathered
for two years rather than the proposed one.

One resident asked about the timeline for sidewalk repairs on Old Mill Lane.
The Town Manager noted the ADA transition plan schedules that block for FY2028
and offered to meet separately.

One resident raised drainage on Dredge Road following the July 9 storm. Public
Works reported the inlet had been cleared July 11.

## Presentations

**Solid waste contract award.** Staff presented the evaluation of four bids for
townwide residential collection. Sedgemere Sanitation scored highest on both cost
and service plan.

## Old business

**Ordinance 2026-11, first reading** — short-term rental licensing. Introduced
and advanced to second reading, 5–0. Vice President Villanueva-Park asked staff
to return with data on how many current listings would exceed the proposed
occupancy limit.

## New business

**Resolution 2026-22** — awarding the residential solid waste collection
contract to Sedgemere Sanitation for five years beginning January 5, 2027.
**Adopted 4–1.** Council Member Brandt voted no, stating he wanted a three-year
term to preserve flexibility.

**Resolution 2026-23** — authorizing the Town Manager to execute a memorandum of
understanding with Sedgemere County for shared dispatch services through FY2029.
**Adopted 5–0.**

## Manager's report

The Town Manager reported the Marsh Landing culvert project was on schedule for
an August 29 completion, that two firefighter-paramedic positions authorized in
the FY2027 budget had been posted, and that the comprehensive plan workshop
dates were set for September and October.

## Council comment

Council Member Sarabhai thanked Public Works for the July 9 storm response.
Mayor Marchetti announced Founders' Day is September 12 on Old Mill Green.

## Adjournment

The meeting adjourned at 9:14 p.m.

*Approved August 18, 2026. Attest: Desmond Achterberg, Town Clerk.*
