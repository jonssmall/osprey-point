## Minutes

**Call to order.** Council President Okonkwo called the meeting to order at
7:00 p.m. All members present.

**Approval of minutes.** The minutes of the May 19, 2026 regular session were
approved 5–0.

## Public hearing — FY2027 budget

The public hearing on the FY2027 operating and capital budget was opened at
7:06 p.m.

Nine residents spoke. Five addressed the proposed water and sewer rate
increase; three of those asked whether a low-income credit could be added. Two
spoke in favor of the additional firefighter-paramedic positions. Two addressed
stormwater investment in the Old Mill watershed, asking that the drainage study
be accelerated.

The hearing closed at 7:52 p.m.

## Budget adoption

**Ordinance 2026-09** — adopting the FY2027 operating and capital budget of
$27,412,600 and setting the real property tax rate at $0.5820 per $100 of
assessed value. **Adopted 4–1.**

Council Member Brandt voted no. In explaining his vote he said the town was
funding the creek and the plant while the neighborhoods that flood first were
still waiting.

Before the vote, the Council amended the budget to add a $40,000 utility bill
assistance fund for households below 200 percent of the federal poverty level,
offered by Vice President Villanueva-Park and adopted 5–0.

**Ordinance 2026-10** — setting water, sewer, and stormwater rates for FY2027.
**Adopted 4–1**, Brandt opposed.

## New business

**Resolution 2026-21** — reappointing Ingrid Sarabhai as the Council's
representative to the Bay Restoration Task Force. **Adopted 5–0.**

## Adjournment

The meeting adjourned at 9:41 p.m.

*Approved July 21, 2026. Attest: Desmond Achterberg, Town Clerk.*
