Marsh Landing Road is closed to through traffic between Bay Street and Pintail
Lane while Public Works replaces the tidal culvert that carries Sedgemere Creek
under the roadway. The closure began Monday, August 10 and is expected to lift
on Saturday, August 29.

## Detour

Eastbound and westbound traffic is detoured via Dredge Road and Old Mill Lane.
Detour signage is posted at both approaches. Drivers should expect to add about
seven minutes to the trip during off-peak hours and up to fifteen minutes
between 7:30 and 9:00 a.m.

## Transit

Route 3 buses are rerouted along the same detour. The Marsh Landing Overlook
stop is temporarily relocated to Dredge Road at Rookery Lane. Riders should
expect delays of 10 to 15 minutes.

## Access for residents and businesses

Properties within the closure keep local access from the Bay Street end. The
kayak launch and the Overlook boardwalk stay open, reached from Dredge Road.
Emergency vehicles have continuous access through a maintained construction
lane.

## Why this work is happening

The existing 1961 culvert has deteriorated to the point that its tidal flap no
longer seals, which allows bay water to push back into the Old Mill drainage
during high tides and coastal storms. The replacement structure is sized for the
projected 50-year storm and includes a new self-regulating tide gate.

The $1.84 million project is funded through the FY2026 capital budget and a
Sedgemere County resilience grant covering 60 percent of construction.
