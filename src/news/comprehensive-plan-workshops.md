Osprey Point has begun updating its Comprehensive Plan, the document that guides
land use, housing, transportation, and public facilities for the next decade.
Three public workshops are scheduled this fall, each focused on a different
theme.

## Workshop schedule

| Date | Topic | Location |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Thursday, September 17, 6:30 p.m. | Housing and neighborhoods | Heron Field House |
| Thursday, October 1, 6:30 p.m. | Waterfront and the Marsh Landing corridor | Council Chambers |
| Thursday, October 22, 6:30 p.m. | Flooding, drainage, and resilience | Heron Field House |

Each session runs about 90 minutes and follows the same format: a short
presentation, then small-group work at tables with maps. No preparation is
needed, and you do not need to attend all three.

## What the plan will address

The 2031 update inherits three pressures the 2021 plan did not fully anticipate:

- **Housing cost.** Median sale price in Osprey Point rose 38 percent between
  2021 and 2025, and the town has added 61 housing units in that period.
- **Tidal flooding.** Nuisance flooding days along Marsh Landing Road have
  roughly doubled since 2015.
- **Commercial vacancy.** The B-1 waterfront district has a 19 percent
  ground-floor vacancy rate, concentrated at the Bay Street end.

## Can't attend?

An online comment form opens September 8 and stays open through November 14.
Written comments carry the same weight as workshop input and are compiled into
the same record for the Planning Commission.

Spanish interpretation is available at all three workshops. Requests for other
accommodations should reach Planning & Zoning at least five business days ahead
at (609) 555-0170.
