---
title: Council adopts $27.4 million FY2027 budget
date: 2026-06-17
category: Finance
lede: The adopted budget holds the property tax rate flat, raises water and sewer rates by 3.5 percent, and funds two additional firefighter-paramedic positions.
---

The Town Council voted 4–1 on June 16 to adopt the FY2027 operating and capital
budget, totaling $27.4 million across all funds. The budget takes effect
July 1, 2026.

## What stays the same

The real property tax rate remains at **$0.5820 per $100 of assessed value**,
unchanged for a fourth consecutive year. Because assessments rose an average of
4.1 percent townwide, the average residential tax bill increases by about $71
even with the flat rate.

## What changes

- **Water and sewer rates rise 3.5 percent**, adding roughly $2.90 per month to
  the average residential bill. The increase funds debt service on the
  filtration plant upgrade.
- **Two firefighter-paramedic positions** are added to Station 2, bringing the
  career staffing level to sixteen.
- **The stormwater fee** is unchanged at $58 per equivalent residential unit.
- **Solid waste collection** moves to a single contracted hauler in January,
  which the budget projects will save $118,000 annually.

## Capital program

The five-year capital plan totals $19.2 million. The FY2027 portion funds:

| Project | FY2027 amount |
| --- | --- |
| Sedgemere Creek filtration plant upgrade | $3.10 million |
| Street resurfacing program | $960,000 |
| Rookery Park field house roof | $415,000 |
| Police vehicle replacement (4 units) | $268,000 |
| Sidewalk ADA transition plan | $180,000 |

## The dissenting vote

Council Member Aloysius Brandt voted against adoption, citing the pace of
stormwater investment in the Old Mill watershed. "We are funding the creek and
the plant," he said during deliberation, "and the neighborhoods that flood first
are still waiting."
