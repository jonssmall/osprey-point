---
title: Community pool stays open weekends through September 20
date: 2026-08-06
category: Parks & Recreation
lede: A warm forecast and lifeguard availability let the town extend the season by three weekends past Labor Day.
author: Parks & Recreation
---

The Osprey Point Community Pool will remain open on weekends through **Sunday,
September 20**, three weekends past the usual Labor Day close. Weekday operation
ends as scheduled on Friday, September 4, when the seasonal lifeguard staff
returns to school.

## Extended hours

| Days | Hours |
| --- | --- |
| Through September 4, daily | 11:00 a.m. – 7:00 p.m. |
| September 5 – 20, Saturday & Sunday only | 12:00 p.m. – 6:00 p.m. |

Season passes are honored during the extension at no additional cost. Daily
admission remains $6 for residents and $10 for non-residents.

## Adult lap swim

Early lap swim (6:30 – 8:00 a.m.) ends September 4 and does not continue into
the extension. The indoor pool at the county recreation center opens for lap
swim on September 8.

## Swim lessons

The final session of group lessons runs August 17 through August 28. A limited
number of make-up slots for weather cancellations are available the week of
August 31; call the field house to claim one.

## Closing note

The pool closes for the season after September 20 for the annual drain,
resurfacing inspection, and filter service. Parks & Recreation expects to open
for the 2027 season on Saturday, May 29.
