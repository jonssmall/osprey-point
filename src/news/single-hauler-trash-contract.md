Starting **Monday, January 5, 2027**, residential trash and recycling collection
in Osprey Point moves to a single contracted hauler. The Council awarded the
five-year contract at its July 21 meeting.

## What this means for residents

Households currently contracting individually with a private hauler should not
renew past December. Collection will be billed through the town's quarterly
utility bill at a flat **$21.50 per month**, which is below the current average
private rate of roughly $29.

Every household receives one 96-gallon trash cart and one 64-gallon recycling
cart at no charge. Carts are delivered the week of December 14.

## Collection day changes

Roughly a third of addresses shift to a different collection day under the new
route map:

| Area | Current day | New day |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Harborside | Monday | Monday |
| Pintail | Tuesday | Wednesday |
| Old Mill | Thursday | Tuesday |
| Egret Flats | Friday | Thursday |
| Marsh Landing corridor | Varies | Friday |

A postcard with your specific collection day is mailed in early December.

## Recycling stays every other week

Recycling remains on a biweekly schedule, collected on the same day as trash.
The accepted-materials list does not change. Glass continues to go to the Dredge
Road drop-off rather than the curb.

## Bulk pickup

Each household gets two free bulk pickups per year, scheduled by appointment.
This replaces the spring and fall townwide bulk weeks, which the town found
generated substantial illegal dumping from outside the municipal boundary.

## Questions

Public Works is holding two information sessions in November — dates will be
posted here and mailed with the December postcard.
