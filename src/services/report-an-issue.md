> **For emergencies, call 911.** For a water main break, sewer backup, or road
> hazard outside business hours, call Public Works emergency dispatch at
> **(609) 555-0139**, staffed 24 hours.

## Where to report what

| Issue | Contact | Typical response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pothole, street defect | Public Works, (609) 555-0130 | Hazards next business day; routine within 10 working days |
| Streetlight out | Public Works, (609) 555-0130 | 5–7 business days (utility-owned poles) |
| Clogged storm drain | Public Works, (609) 555-0130 | 2 business days |
| Water main break, sewer backup | Emergency line, (609) 555-0139 | Immediate |
| Missed trash collection | Public Works, (609) 555-0130 | Next business day |
| Tall grass, junk, unpermitted work | Code Enforcement, (609) 555-0171 | Inspection within 5 business days |
| Abandoned vehicle on a street | Police non-emergency, (609) 555-0155 | 72-hour tag, then tow |
| Noise complaint in progress | Police non-emergency, (609) 555-0155 | Dispatched |
| Downed tree blocking a road | Emergency line, (609) 555-0139 | Immediate |
| Dead animal in the right-of-way | Public Works, (609) 555-0130 | 1 business day |

## What to include

The more specific the report, the faster the fix:

- The nearest street address or cross street
- The utility pole number, if there is one nearby
- A photo, if you can send one by email
- Whether the condition is an immediate hazard
- Your contact information, if you want an update

## Code enforcement, step by step

1. **Complaint received.** Complaints may be anonymous, though staff cannot
   update you if you do not leave contact information.
2. **Inspection** within five business days.
3. **Courtesy notice** if a violation exists, with a 15-day correction period.
4. **Notice of violation** if the condition persists, with a compliance date.
5. **Municipal citation** — $100 for a first offense, $250 for repeats, each day
   counting as a separate offense.
6. **Abatement** by the town for serious health and safety conditions, with
   costs placed as a lien on the property.

Most cases close at step three. The goal is correction, not fines.

## What the town does not handle

- **Disputes between neighbors** over property lines, trees, or fences on
  private property — these are civil matters.
- **Utility outages** — call your electric or gas provider.
- **Barking dogs and animal welfare** — Sedgemere County Animal Control,
  (609) 555-0199.
- **Potholes on state highways** — Shore Road and the Sedgemere Bay causeway are
  state maintained; call NJDOT.
