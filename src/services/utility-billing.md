Osprey Point bills water, sewer, and stormwater quarterly. Bills are mailed
within five business days of the close of each billing cycle and are due 30 days
from the bill date.

## Billing cycles

| Cycle | Period covered | Bill mailed | Due |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Q1 | Jul – Sep | Early October | Early November |
| Q2 | Oct – Dec | Early January | Early February |
| Q3 | Jan – Mar | Early April | Early May |
| Q4 | Apr – Jun | Early July | Early August |

## FY2027 rates

Effective July 1, 2026. Water and sewer rates rose 3.5 percent; the stormwater
fee is unchanged.

| Charge | Rate |
| --- | --- |
| Water, base quarterly charge (5/8" meter) | $28.40 |
| Water, per 1,000 gallons | $6.15 |
| Sewer, base quarterly charge | $34.75 |
| Sewer, per 1,000 gallons | $8.05 |
| Stormwater, per equivalent residential unit, annually | $58.00 |
| Late penalty | 1.5% per month on the unpaid balance |
| Returned payment fee | $35.00 |

The average residential household uses about 13,000 gallons per quarter, which
works out to roughly $248 per quarter under the FY2027 schedule.

## Ways to pay

- **Online** — through the payment portal, by card or bank draft. Card payments
  carry a 2.4 percent processor fee; bank drafts are free.
- **Automatic draft** — enroll once and the balance is drawn on the due date.
  There is no fee, and enrollment is the most reliable way to avoid penalties.
- **By mail** — check or money order to Town of Osprey Point, 220 Marsh Landing
  Road, Osprey Point, NJ 08247. Postmarks count.
- **In person** — the Finance window on the first floor of Town Hall, Monday
  through Friday, 8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. Cash, check, or card.
- **Drop box** — the box outside the Marsh Landing Road entrance is collected
  each business morning at 8:00.

## If you cannot pay on time

Contact Utility Billing at (609) 555-0121 **before** the due date. Staff can set
up a payment arrangement of up to six months on a past-due balance without
penalty accrual, and arrangements prevent the service interruption process from
starting.

The FY2027 budget also funds a **$40,000 utility bill assistance program** for
households at or below 200 percent of the federal poverty level. Awards are up
to $350 per household per year. Apply through the Finance Department.

## Service interruption

Accounts more than 60 days past due receive a written notice, then a door
hanger at least ten days before interruption. Service is not interrupted on a
Friday, the day before a town holiday, or when the forecast high is below 32°F
or above 95°F. Restoration after payment carries a $65 fee and happens the same
business day if payment clears before 3:00 p.m.

## High bill? Check for a leak

A running toilet can add 6,000 gallons to a quarter. Read your meter, stop all
water use for two hours, and read it again — any movement means a leak
somewhere on your side. The town offers a one-time leak adjustment credit once
every three years for documented repairs.
