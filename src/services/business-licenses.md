Any business operating within the town limits needs an Osprey Point business
license, including home-based businesses and businesses licensed elsewhere in
Sedgemere County.

## Before you apply

Confirm two things first:

1. **Zoning.** Your use must be permitted in the district. Call Planning &
   Zoning at (609) 555-0170 with the address and a description of the business.
2. **Occupancy.** A new or changed commercial occupancy needs a use-and-occupancy
   permit before the license is issued.

Home-based businesses are permitted in residential districts subject to limits
on signage, employees on site, customer visits, and outdoor storage. Most
professional and craft businesses qualify without a special exception.

## Fees

| Category | Annual fee |
| --- | --- |
| Home-based business | $45 |
| Retail, under 2,000 sq ft | $135 |
| Retail, 2,000 sq ft and over | $260 |
| Restaurant or food service | $310 |
| Professional services office | $150 |
| Contractor or trade | $175 |
| Short-term rental (from January 2027) | $200 |
| Peddler or solicitor, per person | $80 |
| Special event, per event | $60 |

Licenses run on the calendar year regardless of when issued. Licenses issued
after July 1 are prorated to half the annual fee.

## Applying

Submit the application to the Town Clerk with proof of zoning approval, your
state tax identification number, and any state-issued professional or food
service license. Processing takes about five business days once the file is
complete.

## Renewal

Renewal notices go out in mid-November and are due **January 31**. Renewing is
straightforward if nothing has changed — confirm the details and pay. A change
of ownership, location, or use requires a new application, not a renewal.

Late renewals carry a $50 penalty after January 31 and a $100 penalty after
March 1. Operating without a current license is a municipal infraction with a
$250 fine per occurrence.

## Special events

Events on public property, or private events expecting more than 150 people,
need a special event permit. Apply at least 30 days ahead. Events involving
street closures, amplified sound after 9:00 p.m., or alcohol service need
additional review and should be filed 60 days out.

## Closing a business

Notify the Clerk in writing when you cease operating. Fees are not refunded, but
notice stops renewal notices and prevents a delinquency from attaching to your
name.
