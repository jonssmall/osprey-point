> **Changing January 5, 2027.** Residential collection moves to a single town
> contract. See the [full announcement](/news/single-hauler-trash-contract/) for
> new collection days and cart delivery dates.

## Collection days

Beginning January 5, 2027:

| Area | Day |
| --- | --- |
| Harborside | Monday |
| Old Mill | Tuesday |
| Pintail | Wednesday |
| Egret Flats | Thursday |
| Marsh Landing corridor | Friday |

Set carts out by 6:00 a.m. on collection day, wheels toward the house, with at
least three feet of clearance on each side. Bring carts in by the end of the
following day.

## Recycling

Recycling is collected **every other week** on your regular collection day.

**Goes in the recycling cart:**

- Paper, cardboard (flattened), and cartons
- Plastic bottles, jugs, and tubs marked 1, 2, and 5
- Metal cans, foil, and clean aluminum trays

**Does not:**

- Plastic bags and film — return these to a grocery store
- Glass — take it to the Dredge Road drop-off
- Styrofoam, food-soiled paper, and paper cups
- Scrap metal, textiles, or electronics
- Anything smaller than a credit card

When in doubt, put it in the trash. One bag of the wrong material can send an
entire truckload to the landfill.

## Glass drop-off

Glass is collected separately at the Dredge Road operations yard, open Saturdays
7:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. Rinse containers; labels and lids are fine.

## Yard waste

Yard waste is collected weekly, April through December, on your regular day.
Use paper yard bags or an unlidded can marked "yard waste." Branches must be
under four feet and bundled. Loose leaves are collected curbside in November.

## Bulk items

Each household gets **two free bulk pickups per year**, by appointment. Call
(609) 555-0130 or schedule online at least one week ahead. Limit is six items or
roughly a pickup-truck load.

Appliances containing refrigerant require a $25 recovery fee. Mattresses must be
bagged. Construction debris, tires, and paint are not accepted at the curb.

## Household hazardous waste

Paint, pesticides, motor oil, batteries, and electronics go to the Sedgemere
County collection facility. Osprey Point residents may use it free with proof of
address, on the second Saturday of each month.

## Holiday schedule

Collection slides one day later for the remainder of the week when a holiday
falls on or before your normal day. Observed holidays: New Year's Day, Memorial
Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving, and Christmas.

## Missed collection

Report a missed pickup by 10:00 a.m. the following business day at
(609) 555-0130. Crews return within one business day.
