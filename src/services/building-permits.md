Most construction, alteration, and demolition in Osprey Point requires a permit
before work begins. Permits are issued by Planning & Zoning on the first floor
of Town Hall.

## What needs a permit

- New construction, additions, and structural alterations
- Decks, porches, and sheds over 200 square feet
- Fences over four feet, and any fence in a front yard
- Roofing, siding, and window replacement that changes openings
- Electrical, plumbing, and mechanical work
- Swimming pools, hot tubs, and retaining walls over three feet
- Signs, awnings, and exterior lighting in the B-1 district
- Demolition of any structure

## What does not

- Painting, wallpapering, flooring, and cabinetry
- Like-for-like roof shingle replacement on a single-family home
- Fences four feet or under outside a front yard
- Sheds 200 square feet or under on a permanent-free base
- Routine appliance replacement that does not alter connections

If you are unsure, call (609) 555-0170 before you start. A stop-work order and
double fees are far more expensive than a five-minute phone call.

## Coastal review

Osprey Point lies entirely within the CAFRA zone, so most development is subject
to review under the state Coastal Area Facility Review Act in addition to the
town permit. Work within 100 feet of tidal water generally requires a buffer
management plan, and impervious coverage is capped at 15 percent of lot area in
the back-bay conservation overlay. Expect an additional two to three weeks.

Anything waterward of the mean high water line — piers, bulkheads, floating
docks — also needs a tidelands license or grant from the state before the town
will issue a permit.

## Fees

| Permit type | Fee |
| --- | --- |
| Residential addition or alteration | $85 plus $8 per $1,000 of value |
| New single-family dwelling | $650 plus $6 per $1,000 of value |
| Deck, shed, or fence | $60 |
| Roofing or siding | $50 |
| Electrical, plumbing, or mechanical | $75 each |
| Demolition | $140 |
| Sign | $95 |
| Buffer management plan review | $220 |
| **Work started without a permit** | **Double the base fee** |

## Review timelines

| Permit type | Typical review |
| --- | --- |
| Trade permits (electrical, plumbing, mechanical) | 2–3 business days |
| Deck, shed, fence, roofing | 5 business days |
| Residential addition | 10–15 business days |
| New dwelling | 20–30 business days |
| Anything requiring CAFRA or tidelands review | Add 10–15 business days |

Incomplete applications are the most common cause of delay. Staff will email you
a deficiency letter, and the clock restarts when the missing item arrives.

## Inspections

Schedule inspections at least 24 hours ahead by calling (609) 555-0172.
Footing, framing, rough-in, insulation, and final inspections are required on
most projects. Do not cover work before it has been inspected.

## Expiration

Permits expire if work does not begin within six months of issuance or if work
is abandoned for more than six months. A one-time six-month extension is
available for $40 if requested before expiration.
