Parks & Recreation programs open for registration on a rolling seasonal basis.
Town residents may register **two weeks before** non-residents.

## Registration windows

| Season | Resident opens | Non-resident opens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fall (Sept – Nov) | August 1 | August 15 |
| Winter (Dec – Feb) | November 1 | November 15 |
| Spring (Mar – May) | February 1 | February 15 |
| Summer camp & pool | March 1 | March 15 |

Summer camp fills fastest — most weeks are full within ten days of the resident
window opening.

## How to register

- **In person** at the Heron Field House, 3 Rookery Lane, Monday through Friday
  8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. and Saturday 9:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m.
- **By phone** at (609) 555-0140 during those hours.
- **Online** through the recreation portal.

Proof of residency is required the first time you register — a utility bill or
lease showing a town address.

## Fees

| Program | Resident | Non-resident |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Summer camp, per week | $145 | $210 |
| Youth league, per season | $75 | $115 |
| Adult league, per season | $95 | $140 |
| Group swim lessons, 8 sessions | $85 | $130 |
| Pool pass, individual | $90 | $140 |
| Pool pass, household | $215 | $320 |
| Senior center program | Free | $40 per year |

## Financial assistance

Households that qualify receive **up to 75 percent off** program fees. Eligibility
follows the free and reduced-price meal guidelines. Apply once per year at the
field house; the application takes about ten minutes and covers every program
for that year.

No child is turned away from a recreation program for inability to pay. If the
assistance amount still leaves a gap, talk to the superintendent.

## Refunds and cancellations

- More than 14 days before the start: full refund, less a $10 administrative fee.
- 7 to 14 days before: 50 percent refund.
- Fewer than 7 days before, or after the program starts: no refund except for
  documented medical reasons.
- If the town cancels a program: full refund, no fee.

Weather cancellations for outdoor programs are announced by 3:00 p.m. on the
field house line. Missed sessions are made up when the schedule allows; they are
not refunded individually.

## Accommodations

Parks & Recreation provides reasonable accommodations, including a one-to-one
inclusion aide for camp and league participation. Request accommodations at
registration or at least three weeks before the program starts.
