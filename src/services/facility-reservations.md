Town facilities may be reserved up to **six months in advance**. Reservations are
taken in person at the Heron Field House or by phone at (609) 555-0140.

## What can be reserved

| Facility | Capacity | Half day | Full day |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rookery Park pavilion | 60 | $60 / $95 | $95 / $150 |
| Marsh Landing Overlook pavilion | 30 | $45 / $70 | $70 / $110 |
| Old Mill Green bandstand | 200 | $110 / $175 | $180 / $290 |
| Ball field, per field | — | $40 / $65 | $70 / $115 |
| Pintail Playfields, per field | — | $40 / $65 | $70 / $115 |
| Heron Field House meeting room | 45 | $55 / $90 | $90 / $145 |
| Heron Field House gymnasium | 250 | $130 / $210 | $215 / $345 |

Rates shown as resident / non-resident. Half day is four hours; full day is
eight. A refundable $100 damage deposit applies to the bandstand, the gymnasium,
and any reservation over 100 people.

## Field lights

Lighted play at Rookery Park and Pintail Playfields is available until 10:00
p.m. for an additional $35 per hour. Requests must be made at least 48 hours
ahead so the fields can be scheduled.

## Non-profit and youth rates

Registered non-profits based in Osprey Point and youth sports organizations
serving town residents receive a 50 percent reduction. Town-sponsored programs
have first priority on all facilities, and league play takes precedence over
individual reservations during the season.

## Rules

- Alcohol requires a permit from the Town Clerk and, above 100 attendees, a
  certificate of liability insurance naming the town as additional insured.
- Amplified sound is permitted 9:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m., and until 10:00 p.m. on
  Fridays and Saturdays at the bandstand.
- Inflatables, tents over 400 square feet, and open flame each need advance
  approval.
- Vehicles stay on paved surfaces. No staking into irrigated turf.
- You leave the site as you found it. Trash goes in the receptacles or home with
  you.

## Events over 150 people

Any event expecting more than 150 attendees also needs a **special event
permit** from the Town Clerk, filed at least 30 days ahead. Street closures,
alcohol service, or amplified sound after 9:00 p.m. push that to 60 days.

## Cancellations

Cancel more than 14 days ahead for a full refund less $10. Within 14 days, half
the fee is retained. If the town closes a facility for weather or maintenance,
you get a full refund or a rescheduled date, your choice.
