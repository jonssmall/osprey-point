Parks & Recreation maintains 148 acres of park land, the Sedgemere Creek water
trail access, and 4.6 miles of shared-use path. The department runs youth and
adult leagues, summer camp, the senior center program, and the Osprey Point
Community Pool.

## Parks and facilities

- **Rookery Park** (42 acres) — Heron Field House, two lighted ball fields, a
  playground, and the trailhead for the Creek Loop.
- **Marsh Landing Overlook** (6 acres) — boardwalk, birding platform, and
  kayak launch.
- **Old Mill Green** (3 acres) — bandstand, farmers market on Saturdays, and
  the Founders' Day festival grounds.
- **Pintail Playfields** (28 acres) — soccer and lacrosse, plus the dog park.
- **Osprey Point Community Pool** — 25-meter pool and wading pool, open
  Memorial Day through Labor Day.

## Programs

Registration opens on a rolling basis by season. Residents may register two
weeks ahead of non-residents. Financial assistance covering up to 75 percent of
program fees is available to households that qualify; contact the field house
for the application.

## Reserving a facility

Pavilion, field, and field house reservations are taken up to six months in
advance. Requests for events expecting more than 150 people also require a
special event permit from the Town Clerk.
