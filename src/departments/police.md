> For emergencies, call 911. The non-emergency dispatch line is
> **(609) 555-0155** and is answered 24 hours a day.

The Osprey Point Police Department is authorized for 22 sworn officers and six
civilian staff, organized into patrol, criminal investigations, and community
services. The department shares a dispatch center with Sedgemere County.

## Community services

- **Vacation house checks** — officers will check your property while you are
  away. Submit a request at least 48 hours before you leave.
- **Child safety seat inspections** — by appointment, Tuesdays and Thursdays.
- **Prescription drug take-back** — a permanent drop box is in the Public
  Safety Building lobby, accessible during records window hours.
- **Neighborhood watch** — the community services officer supports eleven
  active blocks.

## Records and reports

Copies of accident and incident reports are generally available five business
days after the report is filed. Requests may be made at the records window or
through the town's records request process. Fees are $0.25 per page after the
first ten pages.

## Transparency

The department publishes quarterly statistics on calls for service, use of
force, and traffic stops, along with its current general orders manual. The
2026 second-quarter report was presented to the Council at the July 21 work
session.

## Accreditation

Osprey Point Police has been accredited by the state commission since 2019 and
completed its most recent reassessment in March 2026.
