Planning & Zoning administers the zoning ordinance, staffs the Planning
Commission and Board of Appeals, reviews site plans and subdivisions, and
enforces the property maintenance code.

## Zoning districts

Osprey Point has nine zoning districts. The three most common:

- **R-1 Residential** — single-family detached, 7,500 sq ft minimum lot, 30 ft
  height limit.
- **R-3 Residential** — attached and small multifamily up to four units, with a
  25-foot front setback.
- **B-1 Waterfront Business** — mixed commercial and residential above the
  ground floor, subject to the Marsh Landing design guidelines.

Because the town sits entirely within the CAFRA coastal zone, most
construction within 100 feet of tidal water requires a state coastal permit and a buffer
management plan.

## Development review

Site plans go to the Planning Commission, which meets the second Tuesday of each
month. Complete applications must be submitted 21 days before the meeting to
make that agenda. Staff issues a written review letter roughly ten days out.

## Board of Appeals

The Board hears variance and special exception requests on the fourth Wednesday
of the month as needed. Filing fees are $250 for a residential variance and $600
for a commercial special exception.

## Code enforcement

Officer Oscar Lindqvist handles property maintenance complaints — tall grass,
inoperable vehicles, unpermitted work, and short-term rental violations. Most
complaints begin with a courtesy notice and a 15-day correction period.

## Comprehensive plan

The 2031 Comprehensive Plan update kicked off in spring 2026. Three public
workshops are scheduled for the fall, and the draft goes to the Planning
Commission next spring.
