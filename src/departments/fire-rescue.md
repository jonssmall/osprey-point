> For emergencies, call 911.

Osprey Point Fire & Rescue operates two stations with a combination of career
firefighter-paramedics and volunteer members. The department answered 1,412
calls in 2025, roughly two-thirds of them emergency medical.

## Stations and apparatus

| Station | Location | Apparatus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Station 1 | 14 Bay Street | Engine 1, Ladder 1, Medic 1 |
| Station 2 | 601 Pintail Lane | Engine 2, Medic 2, Brush 2 |
| Marine | Marsh Landing Overlook | Rescue Boat 1 |

## Marine and water rescue

The department maintains a shallow-draft rescue boat for the creek and the
near-shore bay, and trains jointly with the county dive team twice a year. Water
rescue calls peak between May and September.

## Free smoke alarms

Osprey Point installs 10-year sealed smoke alarms at no cost for town residents.
Call the administrative office to schedule an installation; crews typically
schedule within two weeks. Alarms should be replaced every ten years regardless
of whether they still chirp.

## Volunteering

The volunteer division accepts applications year-round from residents 18 and
older. New members complete Firefighter I or an EMT course, both of which the
department pays for. Members respond from home or hold duty crew shifts at
Station 2.

## Fire prevention and inspections

Commercial occupancies are inspected on a two-year cycle. The fire marshal also
reviews sprinkler and alarm plans as part of the building permit process and
issues open-burn permits between October 15 and April 15.
