The Town Clerk is the official custodian of Osprey Point's records: ordinances,
resolutions, Council minutes, deeds, and the town charter. The office also
administers municipal elections and issues several classes of local license.

## Records and minutes

Council minutes are posted within five business days of approval, and draft
agendas are posted at least 48 hours before a meeting. Records from before 2010
are archived on microfilm and require a written request with about ten business
days' notice.

## Public records requests

Requests may be submitted in writing to the Clerk's office. The town responds
within seven business days as the state Open Public Records Act requires, and
often much sooner. The first two hours of search time are free; after that,
staff time is billed at $22 per hour.

## Elections

Osprey Point holds non-partisan municipal elections on the second Tuesday in May
of odd-numbered years, as its Faulkner Act charter provides. The Clerk certifies
candidate petitions, arranges polling places, and reports results to the
Sedgemere County Board of Elections.

Candidate filing for the May 2027 election opens January 4, 2027 and closes
February 26, 2027. A petition of 50 resident signatures is required.

## Licenses issued by this office

- Business licenses and renewals
- Special event permits
- Peddler and solicitor permits
- Dog licenses
- Pier and mooring registrations

## Notary and certified copies

Notary service is available free to residents by appointment. Certified copies
of town records are $5 each plus copy fees.
