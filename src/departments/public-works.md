Public Works keeps 41 lane-miles of town road, 26 miles of water main, and the
stormwater network that drains the Old Mill watershed into Sedgemere Creek. The
department operates out of the Dredge Road yard and runs a 24-hour emergency
call rotation for water main breaks and road hazards.

## What we handle

- **Streets and sidewalks** — paving, patching, line striping, snow and ice
  control, street sweeping, and sidewalk repair in the public right-of-way.
- **Water and sewer** — treatment and distribution, meter reading, main breaks,
  hydrant flushing, and sanitary sewer maintenance.
- **Stormwater** — inlet and outfall maintenance, ditch clearing, and the
  tidal-flap program along Marsh Landing Road.
- **Fleet and facilities** — maintenance for town vehicles and buildings.

## Reporting a problem

Call **(609) 555-0130** during business hours for non-urgent issues. For a water
main break, sewer backup, or road hazard outside business hours, call the
emergency line at **(609) 555-0139**, which is staffed continuously.

When you report a street defect, note the nearest cross street and, if you can,
the utility pole number. Crews are dispatched on a next-business-day basis for
hazards and within ten working days for routine repairs.

## Seasonal operations

| Season | Operation | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| November – March | Snow and ice control | Priority routes are cleared first; see the snow route map |
| April – May | Street sweeping | Posted parking restrictions apply block by block |
| May – September | Hydrant flushing | Brief discoloration is normal; run cold taps to clear |
| October – December | Leaf collection | Loose leaves at the curb, not in the street gutter |

## Capital projects underway

The Marsh Landing culvert replacement is the department's largest active
project, funded through the FY2026 capital budget and a Sedgemere County
resilience grant. Work began in July 2026 and is scheduled for substantial
completion by late August.
